浏览器侧边栏里的两步验证器

GAuth 为开启了两步验证的网站生成 6 位验证码（TOTP / HOTP），不需要手机。

功能

  • 按分组标签管理账号，置顶、搜索、按发行方自动分组
  • 多种导入：网页二维码、二维码图片、摄像头、otpauth 链接、Google Authenticator 迁移码、Aegis / 2FAS / andOTP / Bitwarden / FreeOTP+ 备份文件
  • 导出：加密备份文件、Google Authenticator 迁移二维码
  • 可选的 Google 云端硬盘备份：备份先用你自己设置的口令在本机加密，云端只存密文
  • 在网页的验证码输入框自动填入验证码
  • 可选的解锁口令与自动锁定

About (English)

GAuth is a browser extension that generates time-based and counter-based one-time passwords (TOTP/HOTP) in the browser side panel. It organizes accounts into groups, imports from QR codes, otpauth links, Google Authenticator export codes and backups of other authenticator apps, and can fill codes into verification fields on web pages.

Optionally, you can back up your accounts to your own Google Drive. GAuth requests only the drive.file scope, so it can access only the files it creates itself (a folder named “GAuth 备份”). Backups are encrypted on your device with a passphrase you choose before they are uploaded. GAuth has no servers of its own.

See the Privacy Policy.
