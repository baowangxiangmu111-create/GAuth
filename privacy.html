隐私权政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日

概述

GAuth 是一个浏览器扩展，用于生成两步验证码。GAuth 没有自己的服务器，不收集、不出售、不向任何第三方分享你的数据，也不包含广告或统计分析代码。

保存在你设备上的数据

  • 你添加的账号（发行方、账号名、两步验证密钥）、分组和设置，以加密形式保存在浏览器的扩展存储中。
  • 如果你设置了解锁口令，密钥只在解锁期间保留在内存里。
  • 这些数据只在你的设备上，卸载扩展即被删除。

Google 账号与云端硬盘（可选）

只有你主动点「连接 Google 账号」时，GAuth 才会请求唯一一项权限：

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file：只能访问 GAuth 自己创建的文件，即你云端硬盘中的「GAuth 备份」文件夹及其中的备份文件。GAuth 看不到你云端硬盘里的其他文件。

连接后，GAuth 会通过云端硬盘接口读取该账号的邮箱地址，仅用于在扩展界面里显示当前连接的是哪个账号。

备份在上传前，已用你自己设置的备份口令在本机加密（PBKDF2-SHA256 + AES-GCM）。云端硬盘里只有密文，GAuth 的开发者和 Google 都无法解开。访问令牌只保存在浏览器会话内存中，不会发送到 Google 以外的任何地方。

你可以随时在扩展里「断开 Google」，或在 Google 账号权限页面 撤销 GAuth 的访问权限；云端的备份文件由你自行保留或删除。

GAuth 对通过 Google API 获得的信息的使用和传输，遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的「有限使用」要求。

网页自动填充

为了在网页的验证码输入框里填入验证码，GAuth 会在你访问的网页上检测验证码输入框。它只把验证码交给网页，不会把密钥交给网页，也不会读取、记录或上传网页内容。

摄像头与屏幕截图

只有你选择「用摄像头扫码」或「扫描当前网页的二维码」时，GAuth 才会使用摄像头画面或当前标签页截图来识别二维码。识别在本机完成，图像不会保存或上传。

政策变更与联系方式

如本政策有变更，会更新此页面并修改生效日期。如有疑问，请在 GitHub Issues 提交。

Privacy Policy (English)

Effective date: October 31, 2026

GAuth is a browser extension that generates two-factor authentication codes. GAuth has no servers of its own. It does not collect, sell or share your data with any third party, and it contains no advertising or analytics code.

Data stored on your device

Accounts you add (issuer, account name, 2FA secret), groups and settings are stored encrypted in the browser's extension storage on your device and are removed when you uninstall the extension. If you set an unlock passcode, secrets are kept in memory only while GAuth is unlocked.

Google account and Google Drive (optional)

Only when you choose “Connect Google account”, GAuth requests a single scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — access only to files created by GAuth (the “GAuth 备份” folder and the backup files in it). GAuth cannot see any other files in your Drive.

After connecting, GAuth reads the account's email address through the Drive API, used only to show which account is connected.

Backups are encrypted on your device with a passphrase you choose (PBKDF2-SHA256 and AES-GCM) before upload. Only ciphertext is stored in your Drive; neither the GAuth developers nor Google can decrypt it. Access tokens are kept in browser session memory and are never sent anywhere other than Google. You can disconnect at any time in GAuth or revoke access at your Google Account permissions page.

GAuth's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Autofill, camera and screenshots

To fill codes into verification fields, GAuth detects such fields on the pages you visit. It passes only the one-time code to the page, never the secret, and it does not read, record or upload page content. The camera and a screenshot of the current tab are used only when you choose to scan a QR code; recognition happens on your device and images are neither stored nor uploaded.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Contact: GitHub Issues.
